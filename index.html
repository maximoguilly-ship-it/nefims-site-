How It Works

Three steps. Nothing left to chance.

Every NEFIMS transaction follows the same clear sequence, visible to both sides from start to finish.

Step 1

Agree on terms

Buyer and seller define what's being exchanged and the conditions for release. Both parties confirm before anything moves.

Step 2

Funds are held

The buyer sends payment into a segregated escrow account. The seller is notified funds are secured — and can act with confidence.

Step 3

Funds are released

Once the buyer confirms the conditions are met, funds release to the seller. Disputes go to mediation before anyone loses money.
