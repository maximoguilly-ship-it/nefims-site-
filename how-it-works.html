How It Works

Four steps. Every step visible to both sides.

Here's exactly what happens from the moment a deal is agreed to the moment funds are released — no hidden stages.

For Buyers

You don't pay the seller. You fund the deal.

Step 1

Create the agreement

You and the seller agree on price, deliverables, and what "complete" means. This becomes the terms NEFIMS holds funds against.

Step 2

Fund the escrow

You send payment into a NEFIMS-held account. The seller is notified funds are secured — but can't access them yet.

Step 3

Review delivery

The seller delivers the work, item, or asset. You have an inspection period to confirm it matches the agreement.

Step 4

Approve release

You approve, and funds release to the seller. If there's a problem, you open a dispute instead — funds stay held.

Typical timeline: funding clears within 1-2 business days; release is instant once approved.

For Sellers

Proof the money exists before you lift a finger.

You never ship, deliver, or start work on a promise. You confirm the funds are already sitting in escrow first.

1 — Confirm the agreement

Review and accept the same terms the buyer agreed to.

2 — Wait for funding confirmation

You'll see a "Funds Secured" status before doing any work.

3 — Deliver

Ship the item, hand over the asset, or complete the work as agreed.

4 — Get paid

Once the buyer approves — or the inspection window closes without a dispute — funds release to you automatically.
