Vehicles & High-Value Goods

Meet a stranger with money on the table. Not in their hands.

Private-party sales of vehicles, equipment, and collectibles fail in two directions: buyers sending money for items that never arrive, and sellers handing over goods for payment that bounces. NEFIMS removes both risks at once.

Why It Matters

The two ways private sales go wrong.

Buyer pays, item never ships

Funds stay in escrow until the buyer confirms receipt and condition.

Seller ships, payment reverses

Funds are already cleared and held before the seller releases the item — no chargebacks after the fact.

"Cash only" meetups get risky fast

Move the payment into escrow before you ever meet in person.

The Process

From listing to handoff, fully covered.

Step 1

Agree on the item & price

Both sides confirm exact make, model, condition, and agreed price in the agreement.

Step 2

Buyer funds escrow

Payment is confirmed and held before the seller ships or hands over the item.

Step 3

Inspect & release

Buyer has an inspection window to confirm the item matches the listing before funds release.
