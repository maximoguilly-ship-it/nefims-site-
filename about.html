About NEFIMS

We built the thing people wish existed before they got burned.

Nexus Escrow Financial Management and Services started from a simple observation: most fraud doesn't happen because people are careless. It happens because someone has to move first, with no way to verify the other side will hold up their end.

Mission

Make "trust me" unnecessary.

Every transaction NEFIMS holds removes one moment where someone has to take a stranger's word for it. We don't sell trust — we make it structurally unnecessary by holding funds neutrally until both sides have done what they said they would.

Neutral by design

NEFIMS has no stake in either side of a transaction. Our only job is to enforce the terms both parties agreed to.

Regulated, not just reassuring

We operate under the same custodial obligations as other licensed money transmitters — not a private promise, a regulatory one.

Built for the deal, not the platform

NEFIMS works whether the transaction started on a marketplace, a classifieds listing, or a handshake at a car meet.
