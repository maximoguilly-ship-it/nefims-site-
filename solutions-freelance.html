The Process

Built around milestones, not a single lump sum.

Step 1

Define milestones

Break the project into stages, each with its own deliverable and payment amount.

Step 2

Client funds the milestone

Payment for the current stage is held in escrow before work begins.

Step 3

Deliver & approve

Once the client approves the milestone, funds release — and the next one opens.
